Privacy policy

Video Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload, schedule and update videos on the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • Data used: only the owner's own YouTube channel, to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish times.
  • Storage: the Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone other than Google/YouTube.
  • Other users: the tool collects no data from anyone else.
  • Removing access: access can be removed at any time in Google Account → Security → Third-party connections.

Google API use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Last updated: 25 November 2026.
